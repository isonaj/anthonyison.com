# Anthony Ison's Blog

A personal site publishing articles about software development and DevOps, served from root-level slugs.

## Content

**Post**:
A dated article, listed in the Feed and the RSS feed, published at its own root-level slug.
_Avoid_: Blog, article, entry

**Page**:
A standalone piece of content (such as About) published at a root-level slug but never listed in the Feed.
_Avoid_: Static page

**Tag**:
A topic label attached to Posts, each with its own Feed of the Posts carrying it.
_Avoid_: Category, label, topic

## Reading

**Feed**:
The newest-first list of every Post on the home page, which keeps growing as the reader scrolls until no Posts remain.
_Avoid_: Archive, posts list, index

**Batch**:
A fixed-size, consecutive run of Posts appended to the Feed as one unit.
_Avoid_: Page, chunk
